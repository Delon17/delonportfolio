Who I am

I have a knack for simplifying processes. I leverage visual interfaces and pre-built modules to swiftly create powerful applications — from automating workflows to crafting intuitive user experiences. I'm all about efficiency and innovation in software development.

I started through internships at the Guyana Lands & Survey Commission and the National Data Management Authority, then grew into an Associate Developer at NDMA — building low-code applications on the Frappe Framework and mobile apps with FlutterFlow, coding in Python, JavaScript, HTML and CSS. Today I'm an Assistant ICT Engineer there, working with AI tooling and prompt engineering alongside development.

Alongside work, I'm studying for a BSc in Information Technology (specializing in network administration and technology development). I'm always looking for interesting problems to solve and ways to keep growing as a developer.
